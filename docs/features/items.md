# Inventory

The [Inventory](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Inventory) is where all items obtained during the player's journey through Teyvat are stored in Genshin Impact.

Up to 2,300 unique items can be held at a time, excluding Weapons, Artifacts, and Furnishings which are counted separately. The 2,300-item limit is based on item type, and does not count quantities of each item. (For example, if you have 400 Berries, those 400 Berries count as 1 item and not 400.)

The maximum quantity that a single item can be held at one time depend on the type of item:

- 99,999: Forging Ore and EXP Enhancement Materials for Weapons, Characters and Artifacts
- 20,000: Lucky Coin
- 9,999: Most items
- 2,000: Food, Dust of Azoth, and Normal Boss Drops
- 999: Strange Tooth and Vitalized Dragontooth
- 99: Vial of Adeptal Speed, Consumable Gadgets, Straight Shooter, Floaty Splody, Hot Pod
- 10: Match Invitation Letter
- 5: Condensed Resin
- 1: Reusable Gadgets and most Quest Items (some Quest Items have exceptions to these limits)

# Items

## Food

[Food](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Food) are Consumable items in Genshin Impact. It is the fourth tab in the Inventory. Food can be used to heal characters, revive characters, buff the player's character, or buff the entire party.

| Cooldown       |        |                                                                            |
| Buffs          |        |                                                                            |
| Multiple Buffs |        | 1 buff per group<br>4 groups: Offensive, Defensive, Elemental, and Stamina |

## Potion

[Potions](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Potion) are consumable Food items that can be crafted with Alchemy at a Crafting Bench. They can buff the entire party's elemental damage or increase their elemental resistance for 300 seconds. The effects of these potions do not stack; using a second potion will overwrite the effects of the first one.

# Gadgets

[Gadgets](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Gadget) are utility items that can be used by the player for various purposes. It is the sixth tab in the Inventory.

Some gadgets can be equipped into the Quick-Use slot, allowing them to be repeatedly used with ease, while others can only be used or placed from the inventory.

# Crafting

[Crafting Methods](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting_Method) are permanent systems in Genshin Impact that allow players to obtain certain items out of various starting materials.

Some like Crafting are instantaneous, while others like Forging and Processing have a processing time. Cooking is the only crafting method that features a minigame. Certain Crafting Methods allow for a character to assist the process, and may yield additional products if that character possesses a Utility Passive related to that Crafting Method.

## Crafting Benches

Crafting Benches are used to perform Alchemy and can be found across Teyvat. They can also be created inside the Serenitea Pot as the Alchemist's Crafting Bench.

- [Crafting](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting) is a Crafting Method available at Crafting Benches to perform Alchemy. It allows players to craft higher quality Character and Weapon Enhancement Materials, Weapon Ascension Materials, Talent Level-Up Materials, Potions, and Character Ascension Materials. Gadgets can also be crafted after obtaining their Instructions.
- [Converting](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Converting) is a Crafting Method available at Crafting Benches to perform Alchemy. It allows players to convert materials from one type to another using a catalyst.
- [Mystic Offering](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Mystic_Offering) is a Crafting Method available at Crafting Benches at Adventure Rank 45 to perform Alchemy. Three 5-Star Artifacts can be sacrificed to obtain an Artifact Strongbox, which contains a random Artifact.

## Forging

[Forging](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Forging) is a Crafting Method where Players can use Forging Ores to forge Weapon Enhancement Materials, which are used to level up Weapons. Players can also use Billets (Northlander, Midlander or Borderland) and Forging Ores to forge 4-Star Weapons.
