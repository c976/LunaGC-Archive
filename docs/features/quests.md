# Story quests

Story Quests consist primarily of chapters related to the background and personalities of each playable character or alternatively Event Quest lines. Hangout Events and Tribal Chronicles belong to this category of quests. They are voiced.

In the data dump, they are labeled with `"questType": "LQ"`.

## Story Keys

Story Keys are quest unlock items that players after Adventure Rank 26 can claim every 8 Commissions completed (i.e., 2 days of 4 Commissions). Story Keys will not be earned automatically and must be claimed from the Story Quests menu once eligible. A total of 3 Story Keys can be held at one time, but the counter will still continue to max out at 8, so one is able to immediately be claimed after one has been spent.

- Hangout Event Acts require 2 Story Keys and repeating the same Hangout will not require more.
- Story Quest Acts do not require Story Keys (Since Version 5.4).
- Tribal Chronicles Acts do not require Story Keys.

Random Events and randomly occurring World Quests are random encounters that the player can run into while roaming around the world of Teyvat. Upon completion, the player will gain a random amount of rewards based on region and World Level.

# Special quest modes

## Contemplation

[Contemplation](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Contemplation) is a game mode that requires the player to analyze several pieces of information and draw logical conclusions.

## Focused Experience Mode

[Focused Experience Mode](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Focused_Experience_Mode) is a feature that can be enabled during certain Quests that prevents most interruptions from other quests occupying locations and characters.

## Guiding Notes

[Guiding Notes](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Guiding_Notes) is accessed from the Quest Overview menu and during dialogue unlocked from the Archon Quest A Divergence Leading to Oneself in Song of the Welkin Moon: Act IX - As All Falls to Emptiness onwards.
