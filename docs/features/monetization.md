# Blessing of the Welkin Moon

The [Blessing of the Welkin Moon](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Blessing_of_the_Welkin_Moon) is a US$4.99 (€4.99) 30 day subscription offer in Genshin Impact. Upon buying the blessing, players will immediately obtain 300 Genesis Crystals and will continue to receive 90 Primogems every day they log in for the next 30 days. Failure to log in will result in a day being deducted from the Blessing without the player receiving any Primogems.

It can be purchased multiple times to extend its duration as long as the current duration does not exceed 180 days (6 total purchases). It is not an auto renewing subscription, so it needs to be purchased manually once the duration has expired.

The Blessing of the Welkin Moon - New Moon Blessing of the Welkin Moon - New Moon item can be obtained from limited time Web Events to receive 30 days of Blessing of the Welkin Moon rewards when used.

# Genesis Crystal

They can only be obtained through in-game purchases. These crystals can be converted to Primogems on a 1:1 ratio.

Material id is `203`, but config often uses `mcoin` key instead.

# Primogem

[Primogems](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Primogem) are the premium currency in Genshin Impact.

Primogems can be used to:

- Replenish Original Resin, for 50–200 Primogems to replenish 60 Original Resin.
- Buy Acquaint Fate or Intertwined Fate in Paimon's Bargains or the Wishes menu, for 160 Primogems each.
- Purchase Battle Pass levels for 150 Primogems each level.

Material id is `201`, but config often uses `hcoin` key instead.

# Original Resin

[Original Resin](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Resin) serves as the energy resource in Genshin Impact. Original Resin is used to claim challenge rewards from Ley Line Blossoms, Petrified Trees (Domains), and Trounce Blossoms (Bosses), as well as Event-exclusive challenges.

To note, any use of Original Resin, excluding the conversion into Condensed Resin, will award Adventure EXP at a ratio of 1:5. For example, spending 20 Resin will result in 100 AEXP.

# Paimon's Bargains

[Paimon's Bargains](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Paimon%27s_Bargains) is a shop that offers Wishing Items, Materials, and a rotating selection of Characters and Weapons in exchange for Masterless Starglitter, Masterless Stardust, or Primogems. It resets on the 1st day of each month.

## Starglitter Exchange

All items are exchanged for Masterless Starglitter. The shop offers 2 different characters, 5 weapons (one of each type), a Crown of Insight, and top-quality Character and Weapon Enhancement Materials every month.

### Rotation

There is a fixed rotation of weapons and characters: 2 sets of weapons (Royal and Blackcliff series) every 2 months, priced at Masterless Starglitter Masterless Starglitter ×24 per weapon, and 12 characters every 6 months priced at Masterless Starglitter Masterless Starglitter ×34 per character.

Characters in the current rotation which have reached Constellation level 6 will be marked with a warning banner stating "Obtained the maximum possible amount of Stella Fortuna".

## Stardust Exchange

All items are exchanged for Masterless Stardust, and the monthly limits reset at the same time as other exchanges.

## Purchase with Primogems

2 items: Intertwined Fate and Acquaint Fate.

# Time gatekeeping / FOMO

Genshin Impact uses recurring limits and real-time waits to pace progression and encourage players to return frequently. These systems can make missing a day, a shop refresh, or a limited-time event feel like lost progress or a lost opportunity. Also see [Reset](./activities.md#reset) for the reset schedule.

- **Limited and resetting shop stock:** Many shops cap how many of an item can be bought, and restock daily, weekly, or monthly. Some offers are also limited-time. Players may feel pressure to check shops before stock or an offer disappears; the caps can also stretch out how long it takes to obtain enough materials.
- **Energy and reward limits:** Original Resin regenerates over time and has a cap, while many repeatable rewards require Resin to claim. This limits how much eligible progression a player can do in one session. Primogems and other items can be used to restore Resin, turning the wait into an opportunity to spend.
- **Real-time activities and processing:** Expeditions occupy characters for hours before their rewards can be collected. Weapon forging and Serenitea Pot furnishing creation also take real time. These timers encourage players to start or collect tasks as part of a regular login routine, and can delay access to desired materials or items.
- **Recurring and expiring content:** Daily and weekly tasks, Battle Pass missions, limited-time events, and rotating Wish banners reward or offer things on a schedule. Missing their window can mean waiting for a refresh or rerun, and daily-login rewards such as Welkin Moon Primogems are forfeited if not claimed.

Taken together, these mechanics are commonly criticized as predatory time-gating and FOMO design: they use delayed rewards, capped opportunities, and fear of missing out to build habitual engagement, while some waits can be bypassed or eased with paid currency. This is a design critique; the mechanics themselves vary in impact, and not every timer requires spending.
