# Domains

[Domains](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Domain) are instances located in temple-like entrances in Genshin Impact.

Completing the challenges within most permanent domains (Challenge Domains and Trounce Domains) rewards various items and materials such as Artifacts, Character Talent Materials, Weapon Ascension Materials, Mora, Adventure EXP, Weapon Enhancement Materials, Companionship EXP in return for some Original Resin.

### Event Domains

Event Domains are domains that are available during a time-limited event. Some event domains are accessible in the overworld, identifiable by its unique doorway entrance. Other event domains are accessible via the event menu.

# Expedition

[Expeditions](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Expedition) is a Game System where players can dispatch their characters to gather Materials or Mora.
The Expedition system is unlocked at Adventure Rank (AR) 14 by completing the World Quest Busy Adventurers' Guild.

Expedition locations are unlocked when the Statue of The Seven that covers the location's area is unlocked.

When Expeditions are first unlocked, players can only send two characters on expeditions at once. By raising their Adventure Rank, they can increase the Expedition Limit cap, which increases the number of characters that can be sent out at once.

| Adventure Rank | Expedition Limit |
| -------------- | ---------------- |
| 14             | 2                |
| 26             | 3                |
| 31             | 4                |
| 36             | 5                |

# Ley Line Outcrop

[Ley Line Outcrops](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Ley_Line_Outcrop) are open-world challenges that come in two forms: Blossoms of Revelation, awarding Character EXP Materials; and Blossoms of Wealth, awarding Mora. Both types also award Adventure EXP and Companionship EXP.

# Spiral Abyss

[The Spiral Abyss](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Spiral_Abyss) is a special type of Domain located in Musk Reef which can be unlocked at Adventure Rank 20. It can be accessed using the wormhole in the sky at the edge of Cape Oath, or by directly gliding to Musk Reef from Cape Oath.

The Spiral Abyss consists of two main parts: the Abyss Corridor (floors 1–8) and the Abyssal Moon Spire (floors 9–12). Completing all floors in the Abyss Corridor permanently unlocks the Abyssal Moon Spire. The Abyss Corridor's rewards can only be collected once, but the Abyssal Moon Spire's rewards reset on the 16th of each month during the Moment of Syzygy.

# Stygian Onslaught

[Stygian Onslaught](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Stygian_Onslaught) is a permanent challenge event.

Stygian Onslaught is accessed at Sal Terrae, Liyue. It is unlocked by completing the World Quest Tumult Subduer, which is unlocked by reaching Adventure Rank 20, completing Archon Quest Prologue: Act III - Song of the Dragon and Freedom. Complete the recommended quest Interlude Chapter: Act II - Perilous Trail for the best experience.

In this mode, the player must fight three powerful enemies, either a Normal Boss or Local Legend equivalent in three separate battlefields. The player must also defeat the enemy within a specific amount of time.

# Travelers' Tales and Anecdotes

[Travelers' Tales](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Travelers%27_Tales) is a permanent game system that spawns Anecdotes with playable characters in the open world.

After reaching Adventure Rank 28 and completing the Archon Quest Chapter I: Act III - A New Star Approaches, talk to Katheryne to complete the World Quest The Many Matters Learned in One's Travels to obtain the Travel Accounts Travel Accounts to track tales and exchange rewards.

Complete the World Quest Travelers' Tales: Destiny Drawn to unlock Destiny Drawn.

Players can interact with the Icon Destiny Drawn icon at the Grand Narukami Shrine to "make a wish" to meet a certain character. This will increase the chances of unlocking an Anecdote for that character for a certain period of time.‍

# Valiant Chronicles

[Valiant Chronicles](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Valiant_Chronicles) are Local Legend bounties found across Teyvat.

After reaching Adventure Rank 55, Valiant Chronicles can be accessed by speaking to Katheryne.

There are three challenge objectives per Local Legend that provide rewards when achieved, along with Avatar Frames after a certain number of objectives are completed. Multiple objectives for the same Local Legend may be achieved during a single encounter, though a minimum World Level of 9 is required to achieve the third objective.

Some of the Local Legends require completion of specific World or Archon Quests before they can be accessed.

# Miliastra Wonderland

[Miliastra Wonderland](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Miliastra_Wonderland) is a User-Generated Content (UGC) gameplay system in Genshin Impact.

Complete the Archon Quest Knights of Favonius in Prologue: Act I - The Outlander Who Caught the Wind to unlock. It is accessed through the Paimon Menu or the quick access button.

# Edge of Survival

[Edge of Survival](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Edge_of_Survival) is a unique end-game mode in which players can challenge one of three particularly strong Shadow Realms for rewards. Players are given a set time to clear the stage, with points being given upon killing enemies.

# Reset

The daily reset occurs at 04:00 (4 AM), based on the player's server's time zone. The weekly reset occurs each Monday, also at 04:00 (4 AM).

## Daily Reset

On daily reset, the player will receive:

- New Commissions (for players Adventure Rank 12 and above).
- Primogems from the Blessing of the Welkin Moon if it has been purchased.

Also affected by daily reset:

- Elite Enemies with limited spawn points per day will respawn.
- Domains: Domains of Forgery (Weapon Ascension Materials) and Domains of Mastery (Talent Level-Up Materials) rotate.
- Daily Battle Pass missions are renewed.
- Ley Line Outcrops locations randomize.
- Mining Outcrops locations randomize 2 hours after daily reset, at 06:00 server time.
- Iron Chunk Nodes and Common Cooking Ingredients growing in the overworld (does not include the Local Specialties used in some cooking recipes) spawn 4 hours before daily reset, at 0:00 server time.
- Time-gated quests and hidden quests progress.
- The overworld Normal Boss Iniquitous Baptist's elemental combination rotates on Asia server's daily reset, regardless of which server the player is in.

Most shops refresh their stock at daily reset every day, every two days or every three days; this reset can vary from vendor to vendor but also from item to item, even if they are sold by the same vendor.

## Weekly Reset

Affected by weekly reset:

- Weekly Bosses rewards and cost-halving opportunities are reset.
- Weekly Battle Pass missions are renewed.
- Reputation bounties and requests reset.
- Shops selling artifacts are restocked.

## Other Resets

Not all resets are bound to server reset times; most items and common enemies in the overworld appear to respawn based on the time the player has harvested or defeated them.

### Respawns 12 hours after harvest

- All animals and insects considered as Wildlife.
    - Weasel Thieves are an exception and do not seem to respawn at all after they are defeated once.
    - Some animals and insects may reappear earlier after escaped, like Crystalflies and Frog.
- Common Enemies that are not part of an Elite Enemy's group. Common Enemies that are part of such a group will respawn along with the Elite Enemy at daily reset instead.

### Respawns 24 hours (1 day) after harvest

- Investigation points that drop Iron Chunks, Cooking Ingredients, low-grade Artifacts, low-grade Weapons or Mora.
- Destructible crates and barrels that sometimes drop Common Cooking Ingredients.
- Option to toggle World Level downgrade/reversion.

### Respawns 48 hours (2 days) after harvest

- All Local Specialties.
- Flaming Flowers and Mist Flowers.
- White Iron Chunk, Starsilver and Electro Crystal Nodes.
- Investigation points that drop White Iron Chunks.

### Respawns 72 hours (3 days) after harvest

- Crystal Chunk Nodes that are not part of a Mining Outcrop and Amethyst Lump Nodes.
- Investigation points that drop Crystal Chunks.
- Fishing spot populations. (Note: Fishing respawn times are bound to the same conditions as daily reset)
