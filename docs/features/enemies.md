[Enemies](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Enemy) in Genshin Impact are creatures that can be defeated by players. They can be classified by their difficulty type (Common Enemies, Elite Enemies, Normal Bosses, and Weekly Bosses), by their family (Automatons, The Abyss, Elemental Lifeforms, Mystical Beasts, Hilichurls, Fatui, Other Human Factions, and Enemies of Note), or some other form of grouping (e.g., Slimes).

See also [the data dump](./data/monsters.json).

# Common Enemies

Players will often find [Common Enemies](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Common_Enemy) in the open world while exploring, or in Domains and Spiral Abyss. These enemies may occasionally drop Character and Weapon Enhancement Materials. They always drop a varied amount of Mora upon killing them, although usually in small amounts and provide very little Character EXP.

# Elite Enemies

Players will often find [Elite Enemies](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Elite_Enemy) in the open world while exploring, or in Domains and the Spiral Abyss.

These enemies are stronger than Common Enemies and are generally found sparsely throughout the overworld, either with other Elite Enemies or by themselves. Some Elite Enemies share some drops with Common Enemies, but most Elite Enemies generally have their own unique drop of ascension materials.

There are a limited number of spawns per day, which will reset daily according to whatever the Daily Reset Time is for the player's server/region.

These enemies are primarily killed for Weapon Enhancement Materials, as they are required in order to ascend weapons, with higher ascensions requiring larger amounts of high-rarity materials.

Drops from Elite Enemies are capped at 400 per day; after this, any Elite Enemies killed afterwards will not award any experience or drops, and is not restricted to World Level.

# Normal Boss

[Normal Boss](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Normal_Boss) challenges contain enemies much stronger than Elite Enemies, but also provide better rewards for defeating them.

Some Normal Bosses may require the player to progress to a certain point in a World Quest or complete a fairly simple puzzle in order to access them for the first time.

A Normal Boss is usually idle in the center of their arena and will not react to the player unless they get too close or attack it. Some bosses may not be present in the arena, instead having an environmental effect which requires the player to get close to it in order to trigger the fight. The lone exception is the Icewind Suite, which requires players to talk to Maillardet in order to initiate the fight.

Unlike Elite Enemies, once they have been defeated a Trounce Blossom will spawn in the area, and players can use Original Resin Original Resin ×40 to open them to claim their rewards.

Normal Boss enemies reward Adventure EXP Adventure EXP ×200 along with a certain amount of Mora Mora and Companionship EXP Companionship EXP. Each Normal Boss has a unique Character Level-Up Material, which is required to ascend characters. They also drop a small array of 3, 4 and 5-star artifacts, depending on the player's World Level.

After claiming rewards from the Trounce Blossom, the boss will respawn in roughly 5 seconds, to give the player some time to prepare if necessary. If the rewards from the blossom are not claimed, the boss will not respawn unless the player teleports out of the area.

# Weekly Boss

[Weekly Bosses](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Weekly_Boss) are bosses whose rewards can be claimed once per week. After defeating each boss, a Trounce Blossom will spawn where players can choose to spend Original Resin Original Resin ×30 to claim the rewards from the first three weekly bosses of their choice, and then Original Resin Original Resin ×60 subsequently. Availability of rewards and reduced resin costs reset at 4 AM server time every Monday.

Weekly Bosses are generally fought in a Trounce Domain (thus players can teleport to them) apart from Andrius, who is fought in the open world.

These bosses are unlocked by completing Archon Quests or a specific character's Story Quest. Once the player reaches Adventure Rank 40, they can Quick Challenge locked Trounce Domains through the Adventurer Handbook in Single Player Mode if they have not met the requirements to normally access it.

All weekly bosses drop their own specific Character Level-Up Materials, which are used to upgrade Talents above Level 6. In addition to this, weekly bosses drop Adventure EXP Adventure EXP ×300, a certain amount of Mora Mora, Companionship EXP Companionship EXP, Character Ascension Materials, and Artifacts depending on the player's World Level.

In addition, there is a 12% chance to drop a Northlander Billet (for Weekly Bosses in Mondstadt, Liyue, and Inazuma), a Midlander Billet (Sumeru and Fontaine), or a Borderland Billet (Natlan and Nod-Krai) and a fixed 33% chance to drop a Dream Solvent Dream Solvent.

Completing the Weekly Bosses' highest difficulty for the first time yields a one-time reward of one of each of their materials, which is claimed through the Adventurer Handbook.

# Special Enemies

[Special Enemies](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Special_Enemies) are Enemies that are not classified under any of the other Living Being Types (Common Enemies, Elite Enemies, Bosses).

# Wildlife

[Wildlife](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wildlife) are a type of NPC that function similarly to Enemies encountered throughout Teyvat. Most Wildlife will be startled and flee when a player approaches them.

Wildlife can be categorized into three sub-types by their interactability: Uninteractable pets (cats and dogs), wildlife that drop items (birds and beasts), and wildlife that act as an interactable item pick-up (material sources).
