Progression refer to subsystems within Genshin Impact that the player participates in to increase the progress of their account

# Achievements

[Achievements](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Achievement) are in-game goals that will award the player with Primogems once completed. Completing all of the Achievements within a category will award the player with a Namecard style that can be used on their in-game profile (some categories like Wonders of the World and Memories of the Heart continually get more achievements added and do not have a completable percentage rating nor completion reward).

The number of successive achievements is indicated by the number of stars in their icon, for example, if there are 3 star slots in the icon it indicates that the achievement has 3 successive tiers to achieve.

At the time of writing (2026-10-17), there are 1,853 achievements. A minimum feature set for achievements to be considered working is:

# Adventure Rank

[Adventure Rank](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank) (AR) is a player account progression system in Genshin Impact.

Upon reaching certain Adventure Ranks, players will be able to ascend their World Level (WL), either automatically or by completing an Ascension Quest.

The maximum Adventure Rank is 60 and the maximum World Level is 9.

# Experience

[Experience](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Experience) (EXP) can refer to multiple ways of progression in Genshin Impact.

World and Character growth are the central progression systems.

- Adventure EXP increases Adventure Rank.
- Character EXP increases the Level of each Character.
- Wonderland EXP Increases Wonderland Level in Miliastra Wonderland.
- Artifact EXP increases the Artifact level and is used to create Artifact Enhancement Materials from Salvaging Artifacts.
- Weapon EXP increases the Weapons level.
- Each nation has their own Reputation EXP, with Natlan featuring separate reputation EXP types for each Tribe.
- Some events have their own event experience that go along with them.
- Companionship EXP increases the Friendship Level of each Character besides the Traveler. Increasing Friendship Levels gradually reveals new pieces of lore in the character's profile, among other perks.
- Trust is used to increase Trust Rank in the Serenitea Pot Housing system.
- Player EXP is used to increase Player Level in Genius Invokation TCG.
- Extraction Progress is used to create Sanctifying Elixir, and is obtained from extracting artifacts using the Artifact Transmuter.
- Dire Prestige is obtained from Stygian Onslaught, and can be used to obtain additional 5-star Artifacts and Dust of Enlightenment.

# Friendship level

[Friendship Level](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Friendship_Level) is a progression of all playable characters except the Traveler and Wonderland Manekins, representing their bond with the Traveler. This progress bar is raised by obtaining Companionship EXP. All Characters in the Party gain the shown amount equally; removing or adding Characters to the Party does not give the other Characters more or less EXP.

Friendship Levels are primarily used to unlock character stories, voice-overs, and character namecards.

# Reputation

[Reputation](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Reputation), also known as City Reputation, is a progression system tied to each nation. Reputation can be obtained from completing bounties, requests, world exploration, and quests. Rewards include Recipes, Forging Blueprints, Crafting Blueprints, Namecards, and Wind Gliders.

Unlocking the Reputation system in each nation requires Adventure Rank 25 and completing a specitic quest for each region.

When the maximum Reputation Level has been reached for a city, no more Reputation EXP can be obtained, and the Reputation Quests Bounties and Requests cannot be completed.

## Bounties

[Bounties](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Bounty) are weekly challenges that are unlocked by reaching Reputation Level 1 (for Natlan Tribes) or Level 2 (for other regions) in a region of Teyvat.

A total of 3 bounties can be completed each week, which is shared between nations and tribes.

Claiming a bounty's rewards will cause another to instantly replace the slot (except in Natlan).

For a detailed list of Bounties with strength/weakness combos, see [Bounty/List](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Bounty/List).

## Requests

[Requests](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Request) are weekly quests that can be obtained by reaching Reputation Level 2 in a region of Teyvat.

Requests are completed by submitting the requested item(s) to NPCs that put out the Requests or defeating the requested enemy camp.

A total of 3 requests across all nations can be completed each week.

Unlike Bounties, Requests are treated as World Quests and can be navigated to from the Quests menu.

# Serenitea Pot

[The Serenitea Pot](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Serenitea_Pot) or Realm Within (colloquially also known as Teapot, Housing, Homeland, or Player Housing) is a system in Genshin Impact that allows the player to create their own home. Players interact with the system using a gadget of the same name.

Players can request to visit their friend's Serenitea Pot in Co-Op Mode regardless of the World Level. Inside, the player will be greeted by the Teapot Spirit Tubby, who will provide access to various features of Housing.

After reaching Adventure Rank 28 and completing the Archon Quest Chapter I: Act III - A New Star Approaches, the World Quest A Teapot to Call Home: Part I will be unlocked. Complete this quest to obtain the Serenitea Pot.
